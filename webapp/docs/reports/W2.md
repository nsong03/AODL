The IM3 figure at `C = 0.3` is a genuine model difference, not a bug: `aodl` truncates `exp(iCV)`
at `C³`, the `J₁` law carries every order, and the `C⁵` terms are ~7 % of a ghost at `C = 0.3`
(the fundamentals differ by the compression difference, 0.27 %). The test asks the 5 % agreement
at `C = 0.15` and bounds the known difference at 10 % at `C = 0.3`. `weak` reproduces `aodl` at
order 1 exactly; against `aodl`'s default order 3 it is brighter by `(1 − C²/8)⁻⁸ = 1.0947`.

## 5. Physics decisions the documents did not fix

1. **Comb demodulation per pixel.** The `j1` lines are the K-point DFT of
   `T̃(u, τ') = i J₁(C|Ẽ|) Ẽ*/|Ẽ|` with `Ẽ(u, τ') = Σ_n b_n(u) e^{2πi m_n Δf τ'}`,
   `b_n(u) = a_n A_n(t_ret(u)) e^{i phase_n(t_ret(u))}` — i.e. the reference tone's instantaneous
   frequency **at that pixel** and the channel's common chirp are demodulated before the DFT
   (both are common to every line of the channel and cannot change an intensity). Demodulating
   with the beam-centre frequency instead would leak ~2 rad of phase over one comb period
   (`ḟ τ/2 ≈ 0.3 MHz` across the aperture during a 48.5 MHz/ms chirp). Envelope drift within one
   comb period (≤ 1 µs against ≥ 46 µs fades) is neglected; this is exact for every drive
   `plan_motion` makes (common ramp, exact combs) and is documented in `pupil.py`.
2. **Comb reference and bin range.** `m_n` is taken relative to the live tone nearest the ladder
   centre, so an `M`-rung ladder occupies `[−M/2, M/2]` and its first IM3 ghosts fit in `K`
   bins whenever `3·max|m| ≤ (K−1)/2`; otherwise a note says the far ghosts alias (the 10×10's
   11 live rungs need `K ≥ 31`). If the live rungs span ≥ `K` positions, or the residual to the
   comb exceeds 1 kHz, the channel falls back to `weak` with a note (as the WO asks).
3. **Pupil-grid alias limit.** The sampled pupil reproduces the image plus copies at
   `λF/dx`; a spot deflected beyond `λF/(2dx)` (85.4 µm at pupil 256 / span 2.5) folds back. The
   engine computes the farthest line-pair deflection per frame and adds a note recommending
   `pupil_n = 512` — the flagship 10×10 (array extent + 40 µm translate) needs it.
4. **Misalignment geometry** (new conventions, kept in `misalignment.py` and documented there,
   since `aodl.device.conventions` has no misaligned crystal): `e_perp = ẑ × e_axis` (+y for an x
   channel, −x for a y channel); rotation is counter-clockwise about the optical axis; the
   magnification scales sound speed *and* aperture (`D → mD`, transit time unchanged), which is
   what makes the fill mask `s u ≤ m[v(t − δt) − D/2]`; `retarded_time`/`filled` are literally
   `conventions.retarded_time(t − δt, u/m)` / `conventions.is_filled(u/m, t − δt)`, so no sign is
   written in this package. Pointing tilts are summed over the four channels and applied once
   per group (every term contains every channel).
5. **Carrier tilt dropped and reported.** `static_offset = deflection_scale·s·f_c·(R(θ)e/m − e)`
   per channel (10.3 µm in Y for `Bx` at 10 mrad), never rendered.
6. **Second-AOD mismatch sign.** The literal derivation gives the negative of the documented
   bracket; `η` depends on `δ²`, so the documents' form is used and the note is in the docstring.
   The efficiency closure for a B channel uses the A channel's amplitude²-weighted live
   frequency at `t_c` (constant over the aperture for one frame); a channel with no live tone
   reports `η = 0` in `trajectory_efficiency`. `t_us` of `/trajectory_efficiency` are observation
   times `t_c + τ/2` over the whole programmed drive.
7. **Metrics are about the brightest spot** (moments over ±4 w₀ about the peak pixel; `w(Z)`
   scans over the full slice extent), the peak is sub-pixel refined (without it a 0.175 µm
   shift changed the "Strehl" by 0.5 % through pixel phase alone), `peak_rel == strehl` as API.md
   defines both, `n_groups` counts the *rendered* groups and `groups_dropped` the rest.
   Brightness for ranking is the incoherent pupil power `Σ‖L_x‖²‖L_y‖²`.
8. **Intensity scale.** The Riemann weight `dx dy` is kept, so images are on
   `aodl.field.focal.intensity_frame`'s absolute scale (verified to 1e-3); API.md's "own units"
   statement remains true but conservative.
9. **Term cut** 1e-5 (amplitude) before grouping and line cut 1e-3 as specified; a driven channel
   with no live tone darkens the frame (no groups) — the counterpart of `build_terms`'
   empty-window behaviour.

## 6. Deviations / notes for W1, W3, W4

  defaults (cut-off side 4.87 MHz = `δ₃/(2C₂f_tpm|γ|)`, ridge side 7.2 MHz); 27.90 MHz for the 3 dB

  `max_{f_B} η_pair = η_A(f_A)·η_peak`, i.e. B fully re-matched on the ridge.

  (§3 above).
