## 5. API ambiguities resolved (and how)

1. **`peakNormalization`** — the work order's shortcut "max over frames of Σ_spots P·2/(π w_x w_y)"
   would divide a 16-trap array by 16 (every spot rendered at ≤ 1/16 scale, dim red). The movie's
   reference (`aodl.viz.movie._peaks`) takes the max *pixel* of the summed group frames, so the
   implementation sums the peaks of **coincident** spots only (centres within half a 1/e²
   radius — exactly the two coincident main-trap groups of a hand-over that API.md §5.1 now
   describes) and takes the max over spots and frames. Unit-tested both ways.
2. **Exact-frame intensity units** — `/api/frame` and `/api/advanced` `peak` is on the SI
   `intensity_frame` scale (power per **m²**), while the movie's `2P/(π w_x w_y)` with `w` in µm is
   per µm²; the frontend multiplies decoded values by `PER_M2_TO_PER_UM2 = 1e-12`
   (`physics/units.ts`). With that, approximation and exact frame share one scale (§4).
3. **`NaN` in JSON** — `f_inst_MHz` arrives as `null`; the client parses leniently (also accepts a
   literal `NaN`) and the aperture code uses `NaN` internally. Fixture loader does the same.
4. **Exact XZ row** — the backend's `row_y_um: null` snaps on the *uncut* spot list (a fading
   ladder's extra columns pulled it to y = 20 µm for the 4×4 while the movie cut y = 6.7 µm), so
   `fetchExact` passes `row_y_um = spotRow(spots at t)` explicitly: both slices cut the same row.
   The exact XZ z axis is centred on the plane (API.md §5.2) and is ticked as such.
5. **Duplicate `fade_events`** (same instant to 1e-16, W1 §5) — `dedupeFades` (1 ns) before the
   timetable, the playback ticks and the badge.
6. **Simulated x, y, z in the metrics** — the power-weighted centre of the *on-canvas* spots (the
   array centre; shadows excluded) and the tracked z; per-spot power uses nearest-position
   tracking seeded from the frame with the most spots.
7. **Initial cursor** — placed at `t = τ` (aperture full) after loading an example; `t = 0` is a
   correct but black frame. Edits keep the cursor.
8. `simulate.constants` typed as the full `PlanConstants`; `trajectory.seams_us` optional.
9. Dev proxy target overridable with `AODL_API=http://host:port` (default `:8000` as specified).
10. Exact XY image is tinted uniformly by the plane's Z (a single intensity image has no per-group
    z); the exact XZ is tinted row by row — both labelled "closed form (Eq. S11)".
